My Projects

Mini Gauge – Hyundai Coupé

Swapping the factory radio for a 2DIN head unit in a Hyundai Coupé GK FL leaves no room for the original multi-gauge. It physically stops fitting. I built a replacement around an ESP32 and a 1.28-inch round IPS display.

Battery voltage is a weighted average of the ADC reading and the CAN bus, coolant temperature comes from the Siemens SIMK43 ECU, and fuel use is calculated as L/100 km on the move and L/h at idle. The LVGL interface runs at 60 FPS and switches to night mode by itself.

MyCarWallet

I wanted to track fuel costs and service intervals for several cars at once without pushing the data to someone else's server. Everything stays in a local SQLite database and the app works with no connection at all.

Receipts are scanned with Google ML Kit OCR, service reminders are worked out in the app, and consumption charts are drawn with fl_chart. Written in Flutter, structured along Clean Architecture lines.
